# Bear Hunt Tracker

A simple way to keep track of Bear Hunts in Kingshot.

The tracker was built to help alliance leaders answer a few important questions:

* Who is showing up consistently?
* Who is carrying damage?
* Is our hunt strength improving?
* Who deserves a promotion?
* Which Front Row players are becoming unreliable?

Everything is stored locally and can be shared between leaders using JSON exports.

---

# What Can It Do?

## Track Every Hunt

Create a new hunt in seconds and keep a history of previous events.

When creating a new hunt you can:

* Copy the previous roster
* Keep player row assignments
* Reset attendance and damage
* Continue tracking without rebuilding everything

---

## Attendance Tracking

For every player you can record:

* Expected attendance
* Joined status
* Damage dealt
* Rank achieved
* Current row placement

This gives a clear picture of who is participating and who isn't.

---

## Top Contributors

Highlights the players who consistently turn up and perform.

Rather than looking at a single hunt, the tracker looks across recent hunts to identify players who are contributing the most over time.

Perfect for:

* Recognising top performers
* Identifying reliable rally leaders
* Rewarding consistency

---

## Front Row Watch List

Front Row players are usually your strongest and most important rally starters.

The Watch List keeps a running tally of missed hunts so you can quickly see:

* Who keeps missing events
* Attendance percentages
* Recent attendance issues

This helps leadership identify potential reliability concerns before they become a bigger problem.

---

## Hunt Strength

How strong was the hunt overall?

The tracker calculates:

* Median Damage
* Average Damage
* Total Damage
* Total Joined Players

This provides a quick snapshot of the overall quality of a hunt and makes it easy to compare one week against another.

---

## Promotion Candidates

One of the most useful sections in the tracker.

Promotion Candidates highlights Second Row and Third Row players who are performing at or above Front Row standards.

This helps identify players who may deserve:

* More responsibility
* Rally leadership opportunities
* Promotion into a higher row

Sometimes the strongest players aren't always sitting in the front.

---

## Damage Trends

See who's improving and who's slipping.

The tracker compares recent hunts and shows whether a player's damage is:

* Increasing
* Decreasing
* Remaining consistent

This makes it easier to spot emerging players before everyone else notices.

---

## Player History

Every hunt contributes to a player's history.

You can view:

* Hunts joined
* Missed hunts
* Attendance percentage
* Other joins
* Average damage
* Long-term performance trends

Useful when making promotion or roster decisions.

---

## Hunt Notes

Each hunt includes a notes section for anything worth remembering.

Examples:

* Rally strategy changes
* Topper-down testing
* Attendance issues
* Rally fill problems
* Leadership observations

---

# Getting Started

## Creating a Hunt

1. Click **+ Hunt**
2. Choose the hunt date
3. Leave **Copy Previous Roster** enabled if required
4. Click **Create Hunt**

The tracker will automatically copy the previous roster and reset attendance and damage fields.

---

## Recording Attendance

### Joined

Expected: ✔
Joined: ✔

### Missed

Expected: ✔
Joined: ✖

### Not Expected

Expected: ✖

These players won't count against attendance.

## Recording Damage

For players that joined, enter:

* Damage
* Rank

Both fields are optional.

---

# Sharing Data

### Export Everything

Use **Export All** to create a backup or share the full tracker with another leader.

### Export a Single Hunt

Select a hunt and use **Export Hunt**.

### Import Data

Use **Import** and select one or more JSON files.

The tracker will automatically:

* Merge hunts
* Remove duplicates
* Keep the most complete version of each hunt

No manual merging required.

---

# Mobile Support

The tracker works on:

* Desktop browsers
* Android browsers
* iPhone and iPad browsers

If opening directly from Discord or another messaging app doesn't work, download the HTML file first and then open it in your browser.

# Why This Exists

Bear Hunts generate a lot of information, but most alliances don't have a good way to track it over time.
The goal of the Bear Hunt Tracker is to make leadership decisions easier by turning hunt data into useful insights.
Reward contributors.
Build stronger hunts.
